# Plans

Plan records and content, approval and lifecycle, validation, agent plan and validation tools, the task link, plan protocol, and the Plans view. Part of [the architecture reference](README.md).

- Plans (durable-plans) are kawai-owned SQLite records of one team (`plans`, `plan_revisions`, `plan_drafts`, `plan_events` in `src/server/plans/`, beside the task and strategy tables): a title, a creator, a stored lifecycle (`open`/`completed`/`cancelled`), and a current approved revision; the human is every plan's owner and only approver, and archiving or killing the creating session leaves the plan. Revisions are numbered and immutable (`in_review`, `approved`, `superseded`, `withdrawn` — superseded and withdrawn ones stay readable), and the partial unique index `plan_revisions_one_approved` makes at most one approved revision a database fact. Content is the template in `src/shared/plans.ts` — goal, success criteria, non-goals, approach, an alignment statement with refs, the ordered changes (OpenSpec name, `dependsOn`, implementing and review group, acceptance, extra checks, guidance), plan-wide acceptance rules and extra checks, the landing mode (`direct`, the default, or `integration-branch` with a branch), the roles (groups with profiles in preference order), a plan review group, an optional Lead role (`leadGroup`, one of the roles; plan-supervision D2), and per change an optional `fixes` list naming the fix or correction task ids it answers (one task may span several changes) — the last two omitted when unset, never `null` or `[]`, so every earlier revision keeps its canonical form and hash — normalized (unknown fields dropped, missing ones defaulted) and hashed server-side: a draft over canonical content, a revision over canonical `{content, pin}`. Saving refuses structural faults with every violation listed (`planStructureErrors` in `src/shared/planChecks.ts`: malformed or duplicate change names, self, unknown, or repeated dependencies, cycles as their path, malformed, duplicate, or floor-reusing check names, a malformed branch, duplicate roles or profiles, groups missing from the roles, a Lead role missing from the roles, a malformed (`task-<uuid>`), empty, or repeated `fixes` entry, duplicate alignment refs); whether changes, groups, or profiles exist is never checked at save. Whether a `fixes` entry names a fix or correction task of the plan (`plan_fix_tasks`) is checked only at read: each miss on the approved or an in-review revision is one `warnings` line on the plan detail (`fixes names no fix or correction task of this plan: <id>`, `fixesReferenceWarnings`), which never refuses saving, submission, or approval. The mandatory check floor (`PLAN_CHECK_FLOOR`: lint, typecheck, unit tests, CI end-to-end, review) is a constant, never content. Drafts are keyed by draft id (default the saving session's id), CAS-saved with `expected_base_revision`/`expected_draft_hash` (a mismatch writes nothing and returns the stored draft), record their latest author, and any team agent may continue one by id. Submission (`plan_submit_for_review`) refuses a closed plan and an incomplete draft (`planSubmitErrors`: goal, a success criterion, a change, every change's implementing and effective review group), pins on the server the team's current approved strategy revision and hash through `strategy_repo_bindings` (or none: Alignment unassessed — never author-supplied), refuses alignment refs the pinned revision does not define (use-case, principle, success-measure, and decision refs; every ref without a pin), returns an `in_review` or `approved` revision with the same hash instead of writing, and otherwise inserts the next revision `in_review` with a `submitted` event; the draft stays.
- Plan approval and lifecycle (durable-plans, `plan-approval`): only the human approves, withdraws, discards, completes, cancels, or reopens a plan — `PlanService` refuses an agent caller first, no agent tool exists for any of them, and a file, message, or draft claiming approval grants nothing. Approval names the exact revision, content hash, and the approved revision the human saw, under a command id: `PlanService.approve` awaits a pre-transaction preparation step (`prepareApproval`, empty for now — the seam later checks that read outside SQLite use), then one transaction replays by `(plan_id, command_id)` (same payload returns the prior result; a different one is refused naming the first differing field), evaluates every entry of the ordered `APPROVAL_PRECONDITIONS` (`planPreconditions.ts`: `plan-open`, `revision-in-review`, `hash-matches`, `approved-revision-unchanged`, `strategy-pin-current` — the pin must equal the team's current approved strategy revision and hash, both empty counting as equal, unless `pinDisposition` returns a recorded disposition — the strategy impact gate's effective `continue`, see the impact bullet) over a context built in one place (`buildApprovalContext`), refuses naming every failing precondition, and otherwise supersedes the previous approved revision, approves this one, sets `current_approved_revision`, and records the `approved` event; a second approved row refused by the partial index rolls back as `approved-revision-unchanged`. Approval and withdrawal write only plan revisions, the current revision, and events — no task, mail, dispatch, tool grant, interruption, or change to linked tasks. Withdraw (`in_review` only; approved and superseded refused), draft discard (records the draft id and last hash), complete (needs an approved revision, plus a reason while any of its changes is not done), cancel (always a reason), and reopen (from either closed state) follow the same command-id replay over their compared payloads (`withdraw`: revision or draft id; `close`: outcome and the trimmed reason; `reopen`: the command alone), each one append-only `plan_events` row with actor, reason, and command id. Closing never touches linked tasks, revisions, or drafts; a closed plan refuses submission and approval until reopened.
- Plan validation (plan-validation, `src/server/plans/validation/`): validating a plan revision — or dry-running a draft — produces an immutable report over pinned inputs, and **approval now requires a current, ready report** (the `validation-current` precondition, appended after the shipped entries in `composePlanServices`'s one production wiring, `planComposition.ts`). The input identity hashes the revision and content hash, the strategy impact state key, each change's resolution (main-root registration, convention worktree only from a listed worktree, exact-date archives), artifact and base-spec digests (lstat, symlink-skipping, 4 MiB cap), the root's OpenSpec config, the named groups' parsed definitions, the known profile ids, other open plans' claims, the adapter's version and capabilities, the checks version, and the record ids — while locations and HEADs stay evidence. `ValidationService.validate` runs single-flight per plan+revision/draft (a dry run defaults the draft to the caller, computes as if submitted, is marked `dryRun`, and stores nothing), gathers file inputs without a transaction, then in one transaction recomputes the DB parts, dedupes on an unchanged hash, and inserts `seq = max + 1`; the OpenSpec adapter (`openspecAdapter.ts`) is the only CLI spawner (probe with a 1.14 floor cached ten minutes on success, `validate --strict --json`, `show --json --deltas-only` with RENAMED as two touches, a 4-slot pool, detached process groups killed SIGTERM→SIGKILL on timeout). The ten technical checks (`validationChecks.ts`) follow the spec-delta relations: two unordered changes touching one requirement conflict (an error; `dependsOn`, directly or transitively, serializes), unordered same-capability pairs raise one overlap warning per capability keyed by the sorted change set, and capacity is evidence only. Agents (`plan_assess`) and the owner record assessments — one alignment value out of five with conditions gated ready/start/acceptance, questions, warning findings; conflicts are sticky until the owner's exception; an empty pin is Alignment unassessed and passes — and only the human decides (answers, acceptances, condition resolutions, scoped exceptions, bounded exploration) through the Plans view's Validation section (`PlanValidationSection.tsx`), each decision idempotent under a command id. Readiness is a pure function (`src/shared/planValidation.ts`): no errors, no unanswered mandatory questions, no unaccepted warnings, and alignment unassessed or passing; approval's `prepareStanding` gathers the file parts before its transaction (no CLI beyond the cached probe) and `standing` compares hashes inside it, refusing as not validated / not ready (with counts) / inputs changed (naming each part). The three tables arrive as migration `plan-validation`, and validation writes nothing outside them.
- Agent validation tools (plan-validation D10, `src/server/plans/validation/validationTools.ts` on the `kawai-team` MCP server): exactly `plan_validate` (a standing revision — newest in review by default, else approved — or a draft dry run; findings capped at 50 with counts), `plan_validation_get` (a report with its standing computed now, plus the revision's assessments and decisions), and `plan_assess` (one alignment assessment and up to 20 questions and 20 findings), allowed without cards, identity-bound, with another team's ids not found. No tool answers, accepts, resolves, excepts, authorizes, or approves, and the prompt's plan note says so.
- Agent plan tools (durable-plans D11, `src/server/plans/planTools.ts` on the `kawai-team` MCP server): exactly `plan_list` (optional status: lifecycle or displayed state), `plan_get` (record, revisions, drafts, events, linked tasks, progress), `plan_create` (title, optional content; refused unless the caller's current group file sets `canCreatePlans: true`, read at each call), `plan_save_draft`, and `plan_submit_for_review`, allowed without cards under either policy, identity-bound to the calling session (unknown arguments stripped), with another team's ids read as not found. No approve, withdraw, close, or reopen tool exists and none may be added; `PlanService` refuses an agent on those paths anyway. The system prompt's plan note names the tools and the creation right, says only the human (every plan's owner) approves and closes in the Plans view, that the approved revision is the direction and drafts or in-review revisions are not, and how `task_create` links a task to a change.
- Plan-change link on tasks (durable-plans D9): `task_create` takes optional `plan_id` and `plan_change`, given together (the human form's `team-task-create` takes `planId`/`planChange`), checked with the task's other references (`src/server/plans/planLink.ts`): another team's plan reads as not found, and a plan that is not open, has no approved revision, or whose approved revision lacks the change is refused naming it. The link is two nullable columns on `team_tasks` (`plan_id`, `plan_change`), fixed at creation like the parent — amending a task, or approving a revision that drops the change, never touches it. Tasks carry `planId`, `planChange`, and `planTitle`; `team-task-targets` adds `plans`: open plans with an approved revision and that revision's change names.
- Plan protocol (durable-plans D12, `src/server/plans/planConnections.ts`, messages in `src/shared/planMessages.ts`): human plan views and commands go over the chat WebSocket addressed by `teamPath` through the shared team-scope resolver (an unknown path is refused as `unknown-team`, another team's plan id as not found, with no data). `team-plans-open`/`team-plans-close` subscribe the list (`team-plans-snapshot`), `team-plan-open`/`team-plan-close` a detail (`team-plan-detail`: the plan, revisions, drafts, events, linked tasks, progress); both are re-sent whole after every committed plan write and every change to a task linked to the plan (`onPlanChange`, fed by the plan store and by the task store filtered by `planId`). `team-plan-approve`, `team-plan-withdraw` (revision or draft id), `team-plan-finish` (complete or cancel with a reason; `team-plan-close` only ends a detail subscription), and `team-plan-reopen` run through `PlanService` as the human with a command id and answer `team-plan-result` (plan, event, `replayed`, or `error` with `reason` and the failing preconditions).
- Plans view (durable-plans D12): the Team page's **Plans** tab (`#/team/<key>/plans[/<id>]`, between Strategy and Tasks) mounts `TeamPlansView` from `src/client/components/team/`; the view is host-agnostic — it owns its `team-plans-open`/`team-plan-open` subscriptions over `teamPath` (ending them with `team-plans-close`/`team-plan-close` when the tab or the plan closes) and re-sends them on reconnect — and the URL names the selected plan, so a reload or link reopens it and a linked task opens in the Tasks tab. One column: the list (title, displayed state, approved revision, strategy pin or **Alignment unassessed**, linked-task count) and, with **← All plans** back, one plan's detail — focus moves to its title — from the approved revision, or until one is approved the newest in-review revision labeled not approved: intent with the author's alignment labeled claimed, not assessed (`PlanIntentSection`); the ordered changes with group, dependencies, acceptance, guidance, and — only for the approved revision — derived status, linked tasks, waits, and tasks outside the revision (`PlanChangesSection`); Kawai's mandatory floor beside the plan's extra checks, landing, roles, and review group (`PlanPolicySection`); each in-review revision's diff summary against the approved one with **Approve revision N** (exact revision, content hash, pin, and the approved revision the dialog showed when it opened, so an approval landing meanwhile makes it stale) and **Withdraw**, drafts with **Discard draft**, every revision, and the event history (`PlanRevisionsSection`); **Complete plan** (an approved revision required; a reason required while changes are not done, which it names), **Cancel plan** (reason required), and **Reopen** for closed plans (`PlanLifecycleControls`). `teamPlanStore.ts` holds one view per team key; every dialog arms one command id when it opens, reuses it while its payload is unchanged (a resend after a lost result replays), re-mints after a payload change, and retires it on success; a refusal turns that dialog's Confirm off with a note to close and reopen it, which arms a new command against the current state; a reconnect clears waiting requests, so a lost result never leaves the view busy. Every control disables while disconnected; refusals show inline with each failing approval precondition. Strategy impact (strategy-revision-impact D6): `team-plans-snapshot` carries `impact` (each plan's group, badge gate — the approved revision's, else the newest in-review one's — and per-revision gates), `team-plan-detail` the plan's, and a `team-plan-result` refused with `strategy-pin-current` the refused revision's `impact` gate, all from the one gate the server approves with; `PlanImpactBadge` shows "Strategy impact: <state>" on list rows and in the detail (nothing for historical plans), a refused approval shows the same state beside its failures, and a non-current gate offers **Open review**, which opens the Strategy tab's impact sub-view on the review's strategy revision. The list and detail are re-sent on every strategy change and impact write as well as plan writes. The New task form adds **Plan** and **Plan change** pickers listing only open plans' approved changes (`team-task-targets`' `plans`); a chosen plan needs its change before create, and while the form is open it subscribes to the team's plan list so each plan push (an approval, a close) re-requests the pickers' targets. Supervision (plan-supervision D15): `team-plan-detail` may also carry `decisions`, `supervision`, and `usage` (the server's supervision seam), and the detail renders each only when present. A card asked from a change shows inline beneath its execution row (`PlanDecisionCard`), the rest in the plan-level Decisions list — never duplicated, so a card is always visible exactly once; **Answer…**/**Override…** open `PlanDecisionDialog` over the store slots `decide:<plan>:<card>`/`override:<plan>:<card>` under the same arming rules, Override naming the decision it replaces (`expectedDecisionId`) and requiring a rationale, both closing on success (the refreshed card arrives in the re-pushed detail) and refusing inline until reopened; the card shows its history with each override's fix task and phase (the button opens the task). A plan or card attention link opens the detail scrolled to and focused on the named change row or decision card (anchors `plan-change-<change>`/`decision-card-<cardId>` over a one-shot `reveal` in `teamPlanStore`, consumed once; an unknown target leaves the detail at its top), the URL staying the plan's (plan-supervision D15). `PlanReportSection` renders `supervision`: outcome counts (never a percentage), "Landed N of M" with its blockers or "All M changes landed" — the success criteria are listed for the owner to judge, never claimed — each stop with its reason label, the last round's evidence and its blocked dependents beside "Next action: Retry or stop execution", failed and superseded candidates, a non-`continue` impact disposition with **Open review**, the overridden-Lead-decisions count, and each landed fix's override-to-landing time; per change it shows the derived outcome, cause, also-applying waits, progress apart from activity with client-ticked elapsed times, and the stall. `PlanUsageSection` renders `usage` — tokens and turns only, never currency — as the total, a group × profile table, one row per change plus one per fix or correction task, and the notes stating what is not counted (task-less turns, Task subagent and auxiliary calls).
