# Plan supervision

Supervision records, decision cards, plan usage, the supervision detail, and the stall monitor. Part of [the architecture reference](README.md).

- Plan supervision records (plan-supervision PR A, §1–4; the decision tables are written through the decision tools of the next bullet and read into the plan detail of the supervision-detail bullet; stall alerts are raised by the monitor of the supervision-monitor bullet): the pure model lives in `src/shared/planDecisions.ts` (decision cards, decisions, fix and correction tasks, the derived card state and fix status, override counts and fix durations), `src/shared/planSupervision.ts` (each change's derived outcome, cause, named `SupervisionWait`s, stall episodes, and failure signature) and `src/shared/planReport.ts` (what stopped and why, `Landed N of M`, never a success claim). Schema migration 16 `plan-supervision` adds `plan_decision_cards`, `plan_decisions` (`UNIQUE (card_id, seq)` arbitrates the first decision and each override; `UNIQUE (plan_id, command_id)` replays a human command, null agent ids never collide), `plan_fix_tasks` (`UNIQUE (source_kind, source_id)`: one fix task per override, one correction per promotion), and `plan_stall_alerts` (one row per `(execution, change, kind, episode key)`; a null `message_id` when the plan has no Lead role). `DecisionStore` (`src/server/plans/decisions/decisionStore.ts`) is the only SQL writer of the decision tables and turns each unique hit into a `DecisionConflict` naming the row that won; `StallAlertStore` (`src/server/plans/supervision/stallAlertStore.ts`) inserts an episode once and returns the stored row on a repeat; every read is team-scoped.
- Plan decisions (plan-supervision D3–D7, `src/server/plans/decisions/`): a plan's optional Lead role (`leadGroup`) decides plan work before the owner. A **decision card** is a question on one plan; its first decision wins (`seq = 1`, kind `answer` or `own-call`), and only the owner replaces it (`override`, `seq + 1`). `DecisionService` writes cards and decisions only through `DecisionStore`, each command in one transaction with the mail it sends — `TeamMailbox.prepareSend`/`prepareReply` run their checks first and commit inside it, so a refusal at any step writes nothing. Two `kawai-team` tools, allowed without cards and identity-bound: `decision_ask { question (1–2000), options? (≤ 8, 1–300 each), context? (≤ 4000), plan_id? }` resolves the plan from the caller's session, never from a supplied task id — a task linked to a plan (its change, and the execution's revision for a Kawai execution task, else the current approved one; a conflicting `plan_id` refused), else the plan's fix or correction task (no change), else the Lead role naming `plan_id` — and anything else is refused (`decision_ask needs plan work: …`); a caller outside the plan's lead group addresses the card to `lead` and, in the same transaction, sends normal group mail to that group tagged `decision-question` with no task (the card carries it, so the worktree-scoped Lead is neither spawned in the dev's worktree nor moved onto its task), else the card goes to the owner with no mail; it returns `{ card_id, addressed_to, addressed_group?, guidance }` (the answer arrives as mail in the thread; do not wait in a loop), and a retry may duplicate a card. `decision_record { plan_id, card_id?, …, choice? | option_index? (1-based), rationale, affected_tasks? }` answers a card addressed to the caller's group (an owner card is refused) — a repeat of the same session's same choice replays, anything else on a decided card is refused naming the decider — and sends the answer through the reply path in the question's thread, on the card's task, tagged `decision-answer`; without `card_id` it records the Lead's own call (Lead role required, a card with no mail). The owner answers and overrides through `DecisionService.answer`/`override` (`ownerDecisions.ts`), human only and replayed by `(plan, commandId)` naming the first differing field: an answer cancels the question mail while queued or awaiting a request (`answered by the owner`) and sends human direct mail to the asker in the thread (else the asker's group); an override needs a decided card and its current decision as `expectedDecisionId` (stale refused naming it), refuses a cancelled plan (`reopen the plan first`), reopens a completed one through `PlanService.reopenWithin` (`override:<commandId>:reopen`), cancels an earlier override's open fix task with a note, and creates a **fix task** — `Forward fix: <question, 80 chars> — <plan>`, a direction naming the card, both decisions, and the affected tasks' result commits and landings since, an instruction to land an OpenSpec change listing the task in `fixes`, no plan link, worktree, or change, a `plan_fix_tasks` row — dispatched to the Lead role with its profiles, tag `plan-fix`, and request key `fix:<decisionId>` (nothing dispatched without a Lead role). The system task caller carries an `origin` (`execution`, `override`, `promotion`) with per-origin rights: only `ExecutionService` mints the execution origin and only `fixTasks.ts` the decision origins (unexported), which may only create an unlinked task with its fix row, dispatch it, and close it as its creator; a structural test holds every other source free of a system caller. A reply to a `decision-question` mail whose card has no decision yet is refused with nothing queued (`decisionReplyGuard.ts`, the `replyGuard` option of `TeamMailbox`, wired in `SessionMailbox` so both the `reply` tool and the human composer meet it): an agent is pointed at `decision_record` with the plan and card ids, the human at the card's Answer in the Plans view; once the card is decided replies pass, and `DecisionService`'s own answers bypass it. The owner's answer and override reach the server as `team-plan-decide` and `team-plan-override` over the chat WebSocket (`decisionConnections.ts`, routed by `PlanConnections`, both in `CHAT_CONNECTION_TYPES`), addressed by `teamPath` like every plan command (an unknown path, another team's plan, or a card of another plan refused with nothing written) and answered by `team-plan-result` with `decision` — the card, the decision, and an override's `fixTaskId` — and `replayed`, or the refusal's message and reason. The system prompt's plan note teaches `decision_ask` (never a prose question to the human on plan work) and `decision_record`, the refused reply to an open card's question, the Lead role's `plan-stall` alerts from Kawai, what `plan_get` adds, and that only the owner answers owner cards, overrides, retries, or stops — no agent tool does.
- Plan usage (plan-supervision D14): read-only over team-process-metrics' per-turn `chat_turn_usage` record, which this change never writes. A turn counts toward plan P when its pinned task (the mail's task for a mail-started turn — none when the mail has none — else the session's task at turn start) has `team_tasks.plan_id = P` (execution tasks included) or is one of P's `plan_fix_tasks`; a task-less turn and another plan's or team's task count nowhere. `readPlanUsage` (`src/server/plans/supervision/planUsageRead.ts`) feeds the pure `planUsage` (`src/shared/planUsage.ts`): sums by `(group, profile)` and by change in plan order, each fix or correction task its own row; turns are row counts, steps the sum of `num_turns`. Tokens only, never currency; Task subagent calls are not counted (the view's notes say so); no backfill — usage starts with the record.
- Plan supervision detail (plan-supervision D10, D15, D16; `src/server/plans/supervision/`): `PlanService`'s optional `supervisionOf` seam — set by `composePlanServices` when the team services carry a `SupervisionRuntime` — adds three fields to the plan detail, so `plan_get` and `team-plan-detail` both carry them (another team's or an unknown plan reads none, and without the seam the detail is unchanged): `decisions` (`planSupervisionDetail.ts`: every card oldest first with its decisions, current decision, derived state, each override's fix task with its derived fix status, and the affected tasks' result commits since the current decision), `supervision` (each change's `changeSupervision` from the loader `readPlanSupervision` in `supervisionRead.ts`, over the plan's newest execution — change rows, attempts, rounds with CI and deciding findings, effects, the outstanding instruction, landing facts and slot, open cards, carried conditions, candidates, results — plus `planReport` with the impact gate's disposition and `computedAt`), and `usage` (`readPlanUsage`). Runtime facts come only through `SupervisionRuntime` (`supervisionRuntime.ts`, built by the manager): the instruction holder's record, pending request, workers, background tasks, due direct mail, parked work, cooldown, last task turn end and task mail — a killed holder reads gone — and the instruction's mailbox blocker from the waiting reasons; `waitEndedAt` is the monitor's in-memory wait observation (null without one), and the loader reads every input in one read transaction, so a write committing meanwhile is wholly in the view or wholly out of it. The detail is re-pushed after every decision write (the decision service announces its plan) and every change to a fix or correction task (the composition maps it to its plan through `plan_fix_tasks`); a monitor sweep pushes it when the plan's derived view changed (compared with `computedAt` and live-turn times blanked) or an alert was raised, and only then.
- Plan supervision monitor (plan-supervision D10–D12, `src/server/plans/supervision/SupervisionMonitor.ts`, `monitorComposition.ts`, `stallAlerts.ts`): each change of a plan's newest execution has one derived outcome, first match wins — landed (a landing fact, with `tree_verified` and base CI), cancelled, stopped (reason and detail), waiting before provisioning (a dependency, a stopped dependency, the parallel limit), stalled as `repeating-failure`, else the first named wait — a Kawai effect, `uncertain-delivery`, staffing, parked or a cooling profile, a pending `request`, `decision-card`, `ci`, `member-release`, `condition`, `landing-slot` — with the other applicable waits listed as also applying, else running or stalled as `no-progress`; an unknown cause renders verbatim. Progress (`lastProgress`: a result, a CI pass, a verdict, a passed round, a landing, a passed candidate) is kept apart from activity (`lastActivity`: the holder's turn end, live turn, task mail); turns, tokens, and tasks.md boxes never count as progress. `no-progress` applies only to an agent-held stage with no named wait — an implement, fix, or update attempt whose instruction was delivered (2 h), or a review awaiting its verdict (1 h) — counted from the latest of the delivery, `lastProgress`, and `waitEndedAt` (the last time the monitor saw the change in a named wait, memory only, seeded with the startup time for every running change so a restart may delay a stall by one deadline but never raise one early); an idle holder (no turn, request, background task, worker, or due direct mail; archived or killed counts) gets 15 min from the later of that anchor and its last turn end. `repeating-failure`: the two newest decided rounds failed consecutively with one signature (sorted failing CI names, flaky excluded, plus `review:fail`), whatever the change waits on. Each episode alerts once: the durable key is the ISO time of the later of delivery and progress (`no-progress`) or the second round's id (`repeating-failure`), so a restart during a stall sends nothing while new progress starts a new episode; one transaction inserts the `plan_stall_alerts` row and Kawai's mail (`from_kind: 'system'`, to the plan's `leadGroup` with that role's `allowed_profiles`, no task, tags `plan-stall` and `stall:<kind>`, naming plan, change, stage, holder, last progress and activity, rounds with failing checks, open cards, and suggesting a message to the holder, `decision_record`, or `decision_ask`, Retry and Stop being the owner's) — without a Lead role the row has a null message and no mail is sent; replies meet Kawai's reply guard. One monitor per team (started by `ChatSessionManager` after the execution runtime's boot, stopped at shutdown) sweeps single-flight at startup, on plan, execution, promotion, task, and mailbox notifications, and on an unref'd 60 s timer while the team has a running execution, a running promotion, an open fix or correction task, or a failed promotion still owed a correction (a team with none is unwatched); a sweep derives each open plan, raises new alerts, creates correction tasks — one per promotion stored as `failed:promotion-conflict` or `failed:ci-failed` (never another failure or a cancel), titled `Correct failed promotion: <reason> — <plan>`, naming the promotion, its closed pull request, the conflicting files or the failing checks with links, and the instruction to answer it with a change listing it in `fixes`, created by Kawai under the `promotion` origin with its `plan_fix_tasks` row and its dispatch to the Lead role (request key `correct:<promotionId>`, tag `plan-fix`; unassigned without a Lead role) in one transaction, so the unique source keeps it to one across restarts — closes fix and correction tasks by the D8 rule (a change counts as landed by its landing fact and its definition in the landing execution's revision, outstanding when the current approved revision names the task and it has no landing fact; Kawai closes the task as its creator with a note naming the merge commits once one landed and none is outstanding — a cancelled or already-done task is never touched; a correction also closes once a later promotion of its plan merged, noted with that promotion), and pushes changed views; an error is logged and the next trigger retries, and a sweep never writes execution rows. The report (`planReport`) states what stopped and why (reason labels, last round's evidence, blocked dependents, **Retry or stop execution**), "Landed N of M" with the blockers — never a success claim; "All M changes landed" leaves the success criteria to the owner — failed and superseded candidates, a non-`continue` impact disposition, "k of n Lead decisions overridden", and each landed fix's override-to-landing time.
