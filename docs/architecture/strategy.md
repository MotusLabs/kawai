# Product strategy

Strategy records, evidence, agent tools, approval, the Strategy UI, and strategy impact reviews. Part of [the architecture reference](README.md).

- Product strategy is a kawai-owned SQLite record (`product_strategies`, `strategy_revisions`, `strategy_drafts`, `strategy_evidence`, `strategy_decisions`, `strategy_impact_reviews`, `strategy_repo_bindings`, beside the team tables): one record per product with a human owner, repository bindings (the owning team's git common dir plus its listed worktrees — the unique root binding enforces one product per team), and a current approved revision. Revisions are numbered and immutable: draft content lives in `strategy_drafts` (keyed by draft id — an agent's session id, or `human`; several drafts per product compete, the human picks on approve), `strategy_submit_for_review` promotes a draft to the next numbered revision with status InReview, approval makes it the active Approved revision and supersedes the prior, and withdrawal marks a revision Withdrawn or discards a draft — superseded and withdrawn revisions stay readable, and a draft never replaces the active approved revision. Every revision's content is the structured template (purpose, primary users, needs/outcomes, main use cases, product approach, principles and trade-offs, boundaries/non-goals, success measures, assumptions and evidence, open questions, decisions and revision history) normalized to all sections — prose may be explicitly empty — and hashed over a canonical serialization (stable key order), so approval and CAS compare hashes.
- Strategy claims carry an evidence kind — owner preference, explicit user statement, agent interpretation, proposed choice, hypothesis, validated observation — plus source, observation context or date, and limitations; a validated observation without a source is refused at save. Success measures record outcome, measure, baseline, and target; values that are not known or agreed are the `Unknown` sentinel, and neither the store nor any tool invents a baseline, target, or metric. Submitted revisions write one evidence row per claim.
- Agent strategy tools (`kawai-team` MCP server): exactly `strategy_list`, `strategy_get`, `strategy_save_draft`, `strategy_submit_for_review` — read and propose — plus the two impact tools (`src/server/strategy/impactTools.ts`): `strategy_impact_get` (the current review's assessment and every review's records, or one named review) and `strategy_impact_propose` (one disposition with a rationale for an affected plan; changes no gate), all identity-bound to the calling session like the task tools, with draft saves CAS-guarded (`expected_draft_hash`/`expected_base_revision`; a mismatch refuses the write and returns the stored draft so the caller keeps its text). There is no agent tool that approves, publishes, withdraws, or deletes a revision, or decides an impact review (`STRATEGY_FORBIDDEN_TOOL_NAMES` names `strategy_impact_decide` and `strategy_impact_record` too), and none may be added: the boundary is structural (see the approval bullet), and the system prompt's strategy note states the rights without ever claiming agents can approve or decide — it names the impact tools, says only the owner decides a review, that proposals change nothing until then, and that an asked agent proposes one disposition per affected plan grounded in what changed between the pinned and current revision. An agent saving with no strategy record is told the human captures one; work proceeds without a strategy and none is auto-created.
- Strategy approval is human-only and a runtime record: `strategy_decisions` is written only through the human UI action path (`strategy-approve`/`strategy-withdraw` over the chat WebSocket, actor `{ kind: 'human' }`), and `StrategyService` refuses an agent caller on those paths. An approval binds the exact revision number and content hash, records approval scope and allowed open questions, and updates `current_approved_revision` in one transaction that also supersedes the prior approved revision and creates exactly one `strategy_impact_reviews` row (state `pending`) — nothing else: no Plan, task, mail, tool grant, or interruption. Idempotency is a database fact — `UNIQUE(strategy_id, command_id)` on both decisions and impact reviews — so a retry with the same command id and payload returns the prior result, the same id with a different payload is refused, and a stale state (withdrawn/superseded revision, drifted hash, or a changed active revision under its prior value) is refused as stale; two racing approvals never leave two actives. A repository file, peer message, or draft content asserting a revision is approved grants nothing — only a runtime decision record does. Withdrawal keeps history, decisions, and impact items. The boundary is the same one `team-task-*` human actions have: the guarantee is that no agent tool surface can approve, not that a local process speaking the UI connection protocol can be told from a browser.
- Strategy UI: the **Strategy** tab of the Team page holds the strategy as five full-width sub-views — overview, review of one revision in review, the impact review, the draft editor, and history — beside a one-line status in the page header (approved revision or none, and the count in review; activating it opens the overview, or the review of the newest InReview revision). The overview leads with direction — purpose, primary users, needs and outcomes, main use cases, principles and trade-offs, boundaries, from the approved revision or, until one is approved, the newest InReview revision labeled as not approved — with the record identity, status, approved revision number, hash, and author in supporting detail, and its other sections (success measures, assumptions and evidence, open questions, decisions) collapsed behind toggles; a product with no record shows the capture affordance (`strategy-capture`: product id, title, initial purpose — creating the record through the first save; offered, never a forced wizard, and work proceeds without it). The review lays the diff from the approved revision beside the `Known` / `Assumed` / `Not decided` findings, with the human **Approve revision N** control underneath (exact revision and content hash, approval scope, allowed open questions; one command id per revision dialog so a retry replays) and **Withdraw**. The draft sub-view edits the template through `StrategyDraftEditor` with a section list beside it (claim-kind labels, `Unknown` baselines/targets, impact proposal); its unsaved text is kept per team in `strategyStore` across sub-view switches and through a CAS refusal (the stored draft shows beside it; a later `strategy-detail` push replaces it; **Reload stored draft** adopts exactly that shown draft, so the next save is CAS-based on it), and **Submit draft for review** sends the human draft. History (`StrategyRevisionHistory`) lists every revision (expandable content — superseded and withdrawn stay accessible), the drafts, recorded decisions, and every impact review with its state and recorded decisions, each opening the impact sub-view. The impact sub-view (`StrategyImpactReview`, `#/team/<key>/strategy/impact[/<revision>]`, offered once a review exists; the Plans view's links open it on the review's strategy revision) shows one review — the current one unless the URL names another — with every review listed by state (Needs decisions, Reviewed, Superseded, Withdrawn); the current review shows its affected, unaffected, and historical plans with each standing revision's pin, gate, and unassessed mark, the effective decision with its history, and the newest proposal with the count, while earlier reviews show only their records. **Decide** (`ImpactDialogs.tsx`) takes a disposition and a required rationale, with **Use proposal** filling both and sending the proposal id (a disposition change drops it, a rationale edit keeps it) and the effective decision it showed as the expected predecessor; **Ask to assess** picks a group (preselecting `lead` when the team defines it). Each dialog arms one command id in `strategyStore` (`armImpact`/`impactCommand`: reused while the payload is unchanged, re-minted after a change, retired on success), shows its own refusal, and closes on success; every (re)open of the view drops waiting requests, so a lost result never leaves it busy. Human actions go over the chat WebSocket (`strategy-save-draft`/`submit`/`approve`/`withdraw`/`impact-decide`/`impact-request`, answered by `strategy-result` — `decision` or `messageId` with `replayed` for the impact commands; authoritative `strategy-snapshot`/`strategy-detail` pushes on every committed change, the detail carrying the composed `impact`, and re-sent on every plan write and impact write too). Every control disables while disconnected; refusals and stale errors show inline.
- Strategy impact reviews (strategy-revision-impact, tables from schema migration 2 `strategy-impact`; `src/server/strategy/impactStore.ts`, `impactAssessment.ts`, `ImpactService.ts`; pure model `src/shared/strategyImpact.ts`): each strategy approval's `strategy_impact_reviews` row is a review; the current one is the review of the current approved revision, every earlier one is superseded (or withdrawn with its revision) and shows only its records. The assessment is derived per read, never stored, for the current review only: closed plans are **historical**, an open plan with an `approved` or `in_review` revision whose pin is not the current strategy revision and hash is **affected** (an unpinned revision is marked unassessed), the rest **unaffected**. The owner records one disposition per affected plan — `continue` (keep working on the old pin), `adapt`, or `reconsider` — with a required rationale (trimmed, at most 2,000 characters, refused rather than cut) in `strategy_impact_decisions`: human only (`ImpactService.decide` refuses an agent first), one transaction that replays by `(strategy_id, command_id)` (same payload returns the decision, a different one is refused naming the field), requires the current review and an affected plan of the team, a named proposal of the same review, plan, and disposition, and the expected predecessor (`expectedDecisionId` equal to the chain head); a later decision supersedes the earlier one (both readable), and the chain index `(review_id, plan_id, IFNULL(supersedes_decision_id,''))` makes two racers leave one effective decision — any unique hit rolls back and resolves as the command's replay or as stale naming the decision that committed. Agents only propose (`strategy_impact_proposals`, append-only, no gate). The gate (contract C-I1, one function for every reader): a revision whose pin is current passes; a pin naming a withdrawn direction (or no approved strategy) is **withdrawn direction** whatever was decided; otherwise the current review's effective decision decides — `continue` passes, `adapt`/`reconsider`/none refuse — so a decision under an earlier review stops counting once a newer strategy is approved. Plan approval consumes it as `pinDisposition` (effective `continue` only), leaving `strategy-pin-current` and its message unchanged; under `adapt` or `reconsider` the plan is re-submitted under the current pin. `stateKey(planId)` (C-I2) hashes the canonical gates of the plan's standing revisions. **Ask to assess** (`requestAssessment`, human only) sends, in one transaction with its `strategy_impact_requests` row, one normal-priority human message to a catalog group with no task, naming the review, revision, and each affected plan, and asking for `strategy_impact_propose` proposals; a same-command retry returns the same message, a new command sends again, an unknown group or stale review sends nothing. Impact records trigger nothing else: no plan, revision, task, spawn, interruption, or other mail is written, and already approved plan revisions are never rebound.
